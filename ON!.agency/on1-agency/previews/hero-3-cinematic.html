Common questions

things people ask first.

01

How long does a typical project take?

+

Most full WordPress builds take 6–10 weeks. An audit is 2 weeks. An identity refresh is 3–5. The biggest variable is your decision speed.

02

Do you work with page builders?

+

No. Every site is custom WordPress with ACF Pro and hand-written templates. Page builders are great for the use case they were designed for; my work isn't that use case.

03

Will my team be able to update the site?

+

Yes. The admin is custom-designed to match the front-end. Plain language, sensible defaults, no plugin spaghetti.

04

Where are you based?

+

Vienna. Most work is remote. I'll come to your studio for the kickoff and the launch if it's in Europe and useful.

05

Do you offer maintenance after launch?

+

A 90-day soft-warranty is included on every build. After that, monthly retainer or pay-as-you-go.
