Common questions

things people ask first.

If your question isn't here, write it in the brief.

01

How long does a typical project take?

+

Most projects run 6 to 10 weeks from brief to launch. Larger scopes with design systems or multiple CPTs may take 12 weeks. We scope this clearly in the first call.

02

Do you use page builders like Elementor?

+

No. Every theme is hand-coded in PHP, CSS, and vanilla JS. This means better performance, lower maintenance, and a site that feels like yours.

03

Can I update content myself?

+

Absolutely. We build custom admin panels and Customizer sections so you can change everything without touching code.

04

What does "one-person studio" mean for me?

+

You always talk to the same person who designs, codes, and ships your project. No handoffs, no telephone game.
