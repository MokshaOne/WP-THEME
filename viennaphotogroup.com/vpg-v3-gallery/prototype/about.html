● About VPG

By photographers, for photographers.

Vienna Photo Group started in 2018 as a handful of people who would rather show one good photograph than ten posts. It's now a member-run collective and magazine — a co-production of Raveenthiran and on1.agency — kept by everyone who's in it.

It began with a complaint. The photography we loved was disappearing into feeds — seen for a second, then gone, ranked by a machine that had never stood in the cold to wait for the light. A few of us decided to make the opposite: something slow, something printed, something you could hold.

The first issue was twenty pages, stapled, given away at a café in the 7th. The map was a pin-board. The membership was a spreadsheet. None of that mattered — what mattered was that it was made by the people in it, and credited by name.

What we believe

Seeing is a craft, not a gift. The city is enough — you don't need to fly anywhere to make good work. Feedback beats likes. And the photographer's name goes on the photograph, every time.

How it works now

Members pay a flat yearly fee. That funds the magazine, keeps the map honest, and pays the photographers we commission. There are no ads and no investors. Decisions that matter go to the members. If you want to see how, the membership page lays it out.

A magazine that happens to be a website, run by people who'd rather show one good photograph than ten posts.— The founding note, 2018

Questions

Good to know.

Do I have to be based in Vienna?

No. Most members are in Wien, but the diaspora is welcome — anyone who photographs the city, or carries it with them, can join. The map and the magazine are Vienna-centred; the membership isn't.

Do I need to be a professional?

Not at all. The only requirement is that you take looking seriously. We have members who shoot for a living and members who've never sold a frame — both belong.

What do I actually get for the fee?

Every monthly issue in PDF, the twice-yearly print run, the right to submit to the map and journal, member-only location notes, event discounts and the studio-share directory. The join page has the full list.

Who owns the photographs?

You do. Always. We ask for permission to publish and we credit you by name; we never claim ownership and never sell your work on. Commissions are paid and agreed in writing.

How do I contact you?

Email the desk at hallo@viennaphotogroup.com, or come to a Photowalk — they're listed in the members' calendar. We read everything, we answer slowly.
