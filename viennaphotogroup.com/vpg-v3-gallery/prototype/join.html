Before you join

Fair questions.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, in two clicks. There's no lock-in and no cancellation fee. Everything you've already downloaded — issues, the archive — stays yours.

How does the print run work?

Members get a physical print run twice a year, posted to you anywhere we can reach. Between print runs, every issue lands in your account as a PDF the day it ships.

Can I gift a membership?

Yes — choose "gift" at payment and we'll send a card with the start date you pick. It's a good present for the photographer who has every lens and no patience.

Do students get a discount?

Students and people out of work pay what they can — write to the desk and we'll sort it. No one who wants to look gets turned away over sixty euro.
